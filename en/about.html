Our Story

A Coffee Shop
Built for Temara

Daily Dose Coffee began with a simple belief: the best coffee is the one you share. In a city where mint tea has long been the ritual, we're proud to introduce a new one — Ethiopian single-origin poured slow, Moroccan spice lattes made with local honey, and a counter where everyone knows your name (or at least your order).

We source from women-led cooperatives in Yirgacheffe and roast in small batches just a few kilometres away. Every cup we serve is a conversation between tradition and craft.

Temara is our home. Pull up a chair and stay a while.
